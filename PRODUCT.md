# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Software engineers and technical operators who run Codex or Claude agents and need a trustworthy way to handle blockers, incidents, approvals, interrupted jobs, and authentication handoffs away from the keyboard. The website helps these users understand the hotline's operating model, safety boundaries, tools, and installation flow.

## Product Purpose

Better Call Sol is a bidirectional voice control plane for coding agents. It lets an agent call its owner with a compact, sanitized brief and lets the owner call back to inspect, steer, or interrupt bounded work. The product page should make the system memorable while giving technically precise evidence that decisions, PIN verification, scoped grants, and audit history remain under local policy control.

## Brand Personality

Urgent, theatrical, and exacting. The voice borrows the tension of a ransom note and an evidence room, then earns trust through concrete implementation details and explicit safety rules. It should feel witty and distinctive without making security sound casual.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic polished SaaS landing pages, interchangeable card grids, vague AI-magic claims, cheerful assistant tropes, and visuals that imply a voice model has independent authority. Do not soften the product into ordinary enterprise copy or let the cinematic treatment obscure technical accuracy.

## Design Principles

- Make the need to call instantly legible and memorable.
- Turn safety guarantees into visible evidence, not reassurance copy.
- Preserve the tension between theatrical presentation and engineering precision.
- Show the bounded human decision loop from blocker to verified outcome.
- Keep setup instructions and operating limits concrete enough to act on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic document structure, descriptive labels for visual simulations, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Decorative lettering must retain an equivalent accessible name. Target WCAG 2.2 AA for all interactive and informational content.
